# 3D models

- `nature/` – Quaternius "Ultimate Nature Pack" (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html), license CC0 1.0.
  Downloaded as .glb via the fan viewer github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase.
- `mega/` – Quaternius "Stylized Nature MegaKit" [Standard] (https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html), license CC0 1.0. glTF files as shipped; textures shrunk to 512/1024 px, TwistedTree leaves recoloured from red to green.
- `../vendor/loaders/GLTFLoader.js`, `BufferGeometryUtils.js` – three.js r128 examples (MIT).
