# Sound credits

All sounds in this folder are CC0 (public domain), free to use without credit. Listed here as thanks.

- Gun shots, far-away shots, magazine out/in, bolt, dry fire:
  "The Free Firearm Sound Library" by Ben Jaszczak et al. (Still North Media), CC0 1.0.
  Mirror used: github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary.
  Files were cut into single shots, normalised and converted to mp3.
- Explosions (boom1, boom2, boom_big, boom_far): made for this game from a Mosin Nagant shot
  (same library, pitched down) mixed with "CrunchBlast01" from Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds (kenney.nl, CC0 1.0).
